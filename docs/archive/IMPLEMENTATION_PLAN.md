7. Object-to-room parent relations.
8. Room containment.
9. Observed-to-predicted and predicted-to-predicted AABB conflicts.
10. Crossings of impassable structures without a door.

Remove invalid predicted nodes with a reason. Reject a completion that cannot preserve the observed graph. If all completions fail, use the observed graph plus a geometric frontier fallback and continue exploring.

### 12.2 Held-out calibration

For each matched hypothesis, calculate raw support as the fraction of eight completions containing it. Match by semantic label, parent room, and fixed spatial thresholds.

Use leave-one-scene-out calibration:

- Fit the monotone support-to-probability map on three scenes.
- Evaluate on the fourth.
- Rotate the held-out scene.
- Tune the acceptance threshold and beta only on calibration scenes.

The calibration label is whether the hypothesis matches the fully explored reference under evaluator thresholds. It must not influence online validation decisions.

### 12.3 Risk-sensitive score

Implement:

~~~~text
U(x) = I_object(x) + lambda_room I_room(x) - lambda_distance d(x) - beta R(x)
~~~~

R(x) is the mean of 1 - calibrated support over predicted nodes visible from x, and is zero when none are visible.

Evaluate:

~~~~text
filter-only
calibration-only (beta = 0)
combined calibrated-risk
~~~~

Do not mix calibration data with held-out scene seeds.
