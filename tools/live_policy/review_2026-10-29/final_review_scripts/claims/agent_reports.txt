-- method
Pre-registration read first; sha256 2571a7ef...2f22 matches PREREGISTRATION_BENCH.sha256 (verified). Nothing was edited outside /tmp/cvp_bench/bench/build/. All Python ran with -B, and a find for newer files under /tmp/cvp_review and tools/live_policy came back empty (verified).

States: the audit's 384 stages. For each stage, bench.py imports the audit (audit.py, which imports work_A replay/replay2/fastig) read-only. It rebuilds the 300 first-stage candidates with fastig.Scorer.first_stage, using the logged RNG index and file order, and re-checks the top-10 against pose_log. All 384 matched (verified). The R1/actual pick equals the logged first-stage best on 384/384 (verified).

Yardstick U_wall: the unmapped oracle_sets.json objects (the audit's primary mapped_masks rule, i.e. oracle_eligible without the reach test). An object counts when its xy distance from the candidate is 0.1-4.0 m and the 2D segment to its centre, minus the last 0.15 m, hits no occupied cell of the wall npz. A cell counts as occupied if it is occupied in any of the 0.6/1.1/1.6 m slices. The segment is sampled every 0.005 m, fully vectorised. U_wall_cone is U_wall restricted to the author's first-stage frustum (fastig, CameraConfig) at the candidate's yaw.

U_360 comes from the audit's own functions. Cross-check against the audit's stages.jsonl: U_360 max equal on 384/384, mean difference 0.0, yardstick counts equal on 365/365, n_cand equal 384/384 (verified).

Guess sets per stage:
- actual: the logged files.
- none: each member's track observed graph.
- placebo A/B: live_policy.placebo_graph with default_rng([20261029, run_idx, stage, gid, 1 or 2]).
- oracle: live_policy.oracle_graph with default_rng([20261029, run_idx, stage, gid, 3]) over the scene's oracle_sets objects. 2,841 member draws, 0 fallbacks.
- R6: default_rng([20261029, run_idx, stage, 0, 4]).integers(0, n_cand, 50).
run_idx is the 0-based line in runs.txt. Every key is stored per stage in stages.jsonl.

Rules:
- R1: fastig IG (I_obj + I_room, first-stage camera) - 0.2*d.
- R2: the same after deleting guessed object nodes, and their edges, within 3 m xy of the walked path, then rescoring.
- R3: R1 + 0.5*min(n, 4).
- R4: sum of member support over guessed objects more than 3 m from walked ground that fall in the 12-yaw second-stage view (0.1-4.0 m), with observed structure and door occluders; then - 0.2*d.
- R5: min(n, 4) - 0.2*d.
- R6: mean of the 50 uniform draws.
Ties go to the first index, which is the author's stable sort order.

Regret = max U - U(pick). Normalised regret divides by max U. Bootstrap: stages resampled within run, 2,000 draws, default_rng(0), one resample matrix per stage subset, so every comparison within a subset is paired.

Decision rule:
- (a) on all 384 stages: ratio of mean oracle regret to mean placebo-A regret, with a paired-bootstrap CI of that ratio.
- (b) on the 119 official stages: the CI of mean(LLM - placebo A) must lie wholly below the floor, where the floor is the 2.5th percentile of the bootstrap of mean(placebo B - placebo A) on the same resamples.

Run time: 26 min wall on 7 workers, 9,644 s user + 266 s system, about 2.75 CPU-hours. No stages were sampled.

Not pre-registered, no decision attached: ignoring cells inside the object's own xy footprint raises U_wall's in-share to 0.146 (gap +0.058, CI [0.043, 0.073]).
