## Errata

The paper text and tables above are unchanged. Later work corrected five claims that the paper or its sources make. The full list is in [docs/RESULTS.md](../docs/RESULTS.md#errata).

- **Table I.** The `support_threshold_tau_1.0` row scores within 0.0002 of the observed map alone, because `tau=1.0` keeps 19 of 8,943 predicted nodes. The gain over `official` shows that the guesses hurt the map, not that the filter adds value. See [WRITEUP.md](../tools/live_policy/results_2026-10-31/WRITEUP.md) §9, erratum 2.
- **"Bit-identical at beta <= 1."** This claim about the risk term is false. The combined risk term moved 2, 0 and 3 stages on the three matrix scenes at beta <= 1. See [WRITEUP.md](../tools/live_policy/results_2026-10-31/WRITEUP.md) §9, erratum 3.
- **"Removes about 27 percent of predicted nodes"** (abstract, §V.B). `tau=1.0` keeps 19 of 8,943 (99.8% removed); WRITEUP §9 erratum 2.
- **"Objects 61 to 45, rooms 313 to 138".** These come from the validator, not from unanimity (WRITEUP §9 erratum 4).
- **"Nine scene and seed combinations"** for the Gemini campaign: not verifiable from the repo (docs/RESULTS.md, Errata).
- **The 61-category F1 list (#93), in the project docs rather than the paper.** Where the paper sources say the list is unpublished, that is wrong. The list of 61 labels exists in the author repo's `task_def/` folder (`tyrone_mirror/DEVIATIONS.md`, entry 93).
